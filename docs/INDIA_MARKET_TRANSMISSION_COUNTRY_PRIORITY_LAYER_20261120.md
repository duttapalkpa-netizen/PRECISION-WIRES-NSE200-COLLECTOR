# India Market Transmission — Country Priority Layer
Date: 2026-11-20 (Asia/Kolkata)
Project: PRECISION-WIRES V11.3 / Global Capital Rotation & Relative-Attraction Overlay
Status: RESEARCH PRIORITY SPECIFICATION — NOT A PRODUCTION SIGNAL OR COUNTRY IMPACT RANKING

## 1. Purpose and scope
Create a dedicated India Market Transmission Priority Layer alongside (not instead of) the existing 63-country Q2-2026 IMF BOP dual-leg validation cohort. The cohort answers which countries have synchronized IMF portfolio-flow observations; this layer answers which foreign jurisdictions transmit measurable shocks into Indian equities, INR, rates, inflation, trade, remittances, and sector earnings.

The user's requested first-priority country groups are retained: United States; China; Middle East oil producers including Saudi Arabia and UAE; Japan; and European economies. The layer then expands to additional countries only where an observable transmission channel exists.

This is an execution-priority framework, not a claim that each country has a fixed or universally comparable causal impact. Do not assign numeric weights until India-specific event/rolling tests establish them.

## 2. Research tiers

### Tier 0 — Global financial conditions and largest cross-market transmission
- United States (US): Federal Reserve policy, U.S. Treasury 2Y/10Y yields, DXY, global risk appetite, FPI allocation, IT/services demand, tariffs and export exposure.
- China (CHN): industrial demand and commodity prices; metals/mining; electronics/intermediate imports; supply-chain competition; Asia/EM portfolio rotation.
- Japan (JPN): Bank of Japan policy, JPY, yield differentials, yen-funded carry unwind, global volatility and Japanese capital-goods/auto exposure.
- European Union (EU27 as a bloc) plus separately Germany (DEU), France (FRA), Italy (ITA), Netherlands (NLD): ECB policy/EUR, trade demand, capital goods, autos, chemicals, pharma, refined-product/logistics hubs and tariff/regulatory channels.
- United Kingdom (GBR): Bank of England/GBP, London risk/commodity/financial-market conditions, services exports, remittances and trade.
- Global benchmarks tracked alongside countries: Federal Reserve, U.S. Treasury, ECB, BoJ, BoE, DXY, VIX, Brent/WTI, freight/shipping and global sovereign spreads. These are institutions/benchmarks, not country rows.

### Tier 1 — Energy, oil supply, geopolitical and shipping transmission
- Saudi Arabia (SAU), United Arab Emirates (ARE), Iraq (IRQ), Kuwait (KWT), Qatar (QAT), Oman (OMN), Bahrain (BHR): crude supply/OPEC+ decisions, LNG, refinery flows, Gulf shipping/insurance, remittances, trade and expatriate labour exposure.
- Russia (RUS): crude/product sourcing, discounts, sanctions/payment/shipping risk, fertilizer/commodities and bilateral trade. Track separately from the Gulf because price, sanctions and settlement channels differ.
- Iran (IRN): Strait of Hormuz and regional escalation/shipping/insurance risk; use observable event and freight/oil variables, not speculative political assumptions.
- OPEC+ as a producer-coordination entity and Strait of Hormuz/Red Sea route indicators are tracked as non-country event variables.

### Tier 2 — Trade, electronics, financial hubs and manufacturing supply chains
- Singapore (SGP): Asian financial/FX hub, portfolio allocation and custody/intermediation, trade/re-export, remittances and shipping.
- Taiwan (TWN): semiconductors, electronics components, foundry supply, hardware/IT production chain.
- South Korea (KOR): semiconductors, displays, electronics, steel, shipbuilding, batteries and auto supply chain.
- Hong Kong (HKG): China-linked capital-market gateway, offshore RMB and regional risk appetite; distinguish Hong Kong from mainland China.
- Indonesia (IDN): coal, nickel, palm oil, metals, ASEAN demand and supply-chain inputs.
- Malaysia (MYS): palm oil, electronics/semiconductor assembly, LNG and ASEAN trade.
- Vietnam (VNM): electronics assembly, textiles, footwear and supply-chain relocation/competition.
- Thailand (THA): auto components, electronics, tourism and ASEAN manufacturing.
- Netherlands (NLD) is also retained as a separate trade/logistics/refined-products node within the EU tier.

### Tier 3 — Commodity producers and strategic raw materials
- Australia (AUS): iron ore, coal, LNG, lithium/critical minerals, gold, education/services and AUD risk conditions.
- Brazil (BRA): iron ore, agricultural commodities, energy and global commodity-demand cycle.
- Chile (CHL): copper and lithium supply; copper-price transmission to Indian metals/electrical/capital-goods firms.
- South Africa (ZAF): platinum-group metals, gold, coal, iron ore and precious-metal risk.
- Canada (CAN): energy, potash/fertilizer, metals, CAD and North American demand.
- Indonesia and Russia remain cross-listed in this tier where relevant; do not double-count country exposures in aggregate.

### Tier 4 — Remittance, trade and regional demand satellites
- United States, UAE, United Kingdom, Saudi Arabia, Singapore, Canada, Australia, Kuwait, Qatar, Oman and Bahrain are explicit remittance-source monitoring rows.
- Sri Lanka (LKA), Bangladesh (BGD), Nepal (NPL), Bhutan (BTN), Mauritius (MUS) and Maldives (MDV): regional trade, tourism, labour/remittance, financial linkages and cross-border policy/FX spillovers where measurable.
- Switzerland (CHE): gold/refining/trading, pharmaceuticals, financial conditions and commodity-price transmission; do not infer that all Swiss flows are portfolio investment.
- Belgium (BEL), Spain (ESP), Italy (ITA), France (FRA), Germany (DEU) and Ireland (IRL): EU country-specific export demand/sector exposure, where official bilateral trade data justify separate treatment.

## 3. Evidence supporting inclusion (source-derived facts)
- RBI's 31-Jul-2026 Direction of Foreign Trade table reports FY2025-26 country/region exports and imports. In its North America table, U.S. exports from India were ₹770,280 crore and imports ₹472,725 crore; EU exports ₹622,551 crore and imports ₹580,731 crore. Germany, France, Netherlands and other EU country rows are separately available. Source: https://rbi.org.in/scriptS/PublicationsView.aspx?id=23945
- Department of Commerce TIA portal (DGCIS data; updated through Aug-2026) identifies top export destinations as United States, UAE, China, Netherlands and United Kingdom, collectively 40% of exports; top import sources China, UAE, Russia, United States and Saudi Arabia, collectively 43% of imports. Source: https://trade-analytics.commerce.gov.in/public
- EU Council's 2025 trade fact sheet reports EU was India's second-largest goods-trade partner in 2025, 11.6% of India's goods trade, with bilateral goods trade above €118bn. Source: https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/infographics/eu-india-trade-facts-and-figures/
- RBI remittance source-country survey table reports 2023-24 shares: US 27.7%, UAE 19.2%, UK 10.8%, Saudi Arabia 6.7%, Singapore 6.6%, Kuwait 3.9%, Qatar 4.1%, Oman 2.5%, Canada 3.8%, Australia 2.3%; GCC countries together contributed 38%. These are source-country remittance shares, not equity-flow shares. Source: https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/BS_ViewBulletin.aspx?Id=23260
- RBI research (Jul-2025) estimates India's crude import dependency rose from 77.6% in FY2013-14 to 88.2% in FY2024-25 and estimates a 10% global crude-price rise could add around 20 basis points to inflation under the study's model. Source: https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewBulletin.aspx?Id=23516
- RBI's 2026 analysis documents West Asia energy shocks transmitting through crude import bill, inflation, CAD, rupee pressure, bond yields and capital flows. Source: https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/PublicationReportDetails.aspx?ID=1324
- Economic Survey 2025-26 describes elevated U.S. bond yields/global risk-off sentiment as weighing on FPI flows to India. Source: https://www.indiabudget.gov.in/economicsurvey/doc/echapter.pdf
- PIB/Commerce 18-Nov-2026 trade update reports April-Sep FY2026-27 exports to ASEAN +58% to US$28.06bn and EU +5% to US$38.79bn, with Japan, South Korea, Singapore and Malaysia among named markets. Source: https://www.pib.gov.in/FactsheetDetails.aspx?ModuleId=16&NoteId=151203&id=151203&lang=1&reg=3

## 4. India transmission channels and sector mapping
| Channel | Foreign variables | Indian macro/market variables | Indicative Indian sector sensitivity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Global liquidity/risk | Fed funds, UST 2Y/10Y, DXY, VIX, BoJ/JPY, ECB | FPI equity/debt flows, INR, G-sec yields, Nifty breadth/volatility | Banks, financials, rate sensitives, broad index, high-beta |
| Energy/import bill | Brent/Indian basket, OPEC+ supply, Gulf risk, Russia discounts, freight/insurance | CAD, INR, WPI/CPI fuel, fiscal subsidy, refining margins | OMCs, refiners, aviation, paints, tyres, chemicals, logistics |
| China/Asia industrial cycle | China PMI/credit/steel output, copper/aluminium, Taiwan/Korea chip cycle | Metal prices, input costs, export orders, electronics supply | Metals/mining, capital goods, auto, electronics, cables |
| Trade/tariffs/demand | US/EU/UK/ASEAN orders, tariffs, trade rules | Export growth, corporate guidance, margins, services receipts | IT, pharma, textiles, gems/jewellery, auto components, chemicals |
| Remittances/Gulf | US/GCC/UK remittance and employment cycle | Household transfers, FX supply, consumption, regional demand | Consumer, housing, gold/jewellery, banks/NBFCs (indirect) |
| Strategic materials | Australia/Brazil/Chile/Indonesia/Canada supply | Ore/metal/energy prices, inventory and input cost | Metals, mining, batteries, renewables, capital goods |

These are hypotheses for measurement, not automatic stock signals. Firm-level import/export exposure and hedging must be checked before sector classification.

## 5. Required data contract — country × channel × India outcome
For each country and event, capture:
1. Official source URL, publisher, publication timestamp, reference period, retrieval timestamp, raw bytes and SHA-256 where a downloadable source exists.
2. Policy/market shock: policy rate, 2Y/10Y yield, FX, PMI/industrial production, crude production/export, tariff announcement, trade release, shipping/freight disruption, remittance data or commodity production.
3. India response: Nifty/Sensex return, sector index return, INR, India 10Y G-sec, FPI equity/debt net flow, crude basket, relevant commodity futures and company-level disclosed exposure.
4. Event time alignment: source timezone, India trading session, pre-market/intraday/post-market classification and lag windows H+0, H+1, H+3, H+5.
5. Confounders: RBI policy, domestic earnings, budget/tax/regulatory changes, domestic flows, other simultaneous geopolitical shocks.
6. Evidence state: PASS / PARTIAL / BLOCKED / UNKNOWN. No missing value or unverified source can be PASS.

## 6. Priority execution sequence
1. Establish India benchmark panel first: Nifty/Sensex, sector indices, INR, India 10Y, FPI equity/debt, Indian crude basket, Brent, DXY, UST 2Y/10Y, VIX, gold, copper, aluminium and freight.
2. Build Tier 0 country/benchmark coverage: US, China, Japan, EU/ECB, UK/BoE; collect synchronized official series and event timestamps.
3. Build Tier 1 energy/West Asia: Saudi Arabia, UAE, Iraq, Kuwait, Qatar, Oman, Bahrain, Russia; add OPEC+ and shipping chokepoint event indicators.
4. Build Tier 2 Asia supply chain and finance: Singapore, Taiwan, South Korea, Hong Kong, Indonesia, Malaysia, Vietnam, Thailand.
5. Build Tier 3 commodities: Australia, Brazil, Chile, South Africa, Canada, Switzerland.
6. Build Tier 4 remittance/regional satellites, prioritizing source-country remittance share and bilateral trade evidence.
7. Only after adequate matured event observations, run country/channel-specific event studies and rolling out-of-sample tests. No arbitrary impact weights or country rankings before this.
8. Then estimate sector betas/lag response and test whether the overlay adds predictive value beyond domestic variables.

## 7. Safeguards and invariants
- This India Market Transmission universe is a distinct research layer; do not replace or mutate the fixed 63-country Q2-2026 IMF BOP cohort.
- Country inclusion does not imply that the country has a Q2-2026 IMF dual-leg observation or national validation.
- No paid APIs/subscriptions; ₹0 research spend; free official sources only where possible.
- No synthetic data, fabricated timestamps, annual-to-quarter allocation, stock-as-flow substitution, inferred sign/scale transforms, or silent FX conversion.
- No causal claim from correlation alone; event studies require confounder controls and out-of-sample validation.
- No production selector, RANKING_SCORE, +1 PRESSURE_FADE, CORE-672 (56×12), Engines A–E, IPO-PLX V2, weights, trade gates or alert changes.
- UNKNOWN is never PASS. No production integration without separate acceptance evidence and explicit approval.

## 8. Acceptance criteria for this layer
- Country/benchmark universe and channel map documented: PASS when reviewed and versioned.
- Each country has at least one relevant official data source and a defined India outcome variable: otherwise PARTIAL.
- Timezone, vintage, raw archive/hash and event join QA complete: PASS only with evidence.
- Country-level impact strength: NOT_RANKED until minimum matured observations and pre-registered OOS tests are met.
- Sector impact: NOT_ESTABLISHED until issuer-level exposure and event response are measured.
- Production use: NOT_AUTHORIZED until independent OOS evidence and explicit approval.
